# Reproduced controlled results

All values are generated by the packaged scripts from fixed seeds. They validate the controlled research model; they are **not** court-level legal accuracy.

- Principal benchmark: 16,000 method-trials (10 families × 40 episodes × 8 retention levels × 5 methods).
- TRACE-R: complete-evidence exact attribution 1.000; at 90% retention exact attribution 0.608, abstention 0.393, selective accuracy 1.000.
- Paired identifiability: removing the critical responsibility-separating prerequisite causes 1.000 TRACE-R abstention.
- Static/adaptive capacity: the base architecture collapses under one targeted channel-class deletion; witness hardening preserves mean adaptive capacity ≈0.897 at budget one and collapses at budget two in the modeled architecture.
- Responsibility coding: seven-state repeated code has d_min = 2,4,6,8,10 for redundancy 1–5.
- Mixed error–erasure theorem: tested condition is 2t+b < d_min. A 75-condition software sweep agrees with the theorem for the tested codebooks.
- Minimum-cost coding: the MILP selects the least-cost candidate evidence claims meeting d_min ≥ 2t+b+1. Representative b=1,t=1 requires 7 channels, d_min=4, normalized cost 7.35.
- Identifiability interval: at 80% retention on a deliberately coarse six-hypothesis channel signature, Fano lower bound 0.226, exact MAP error 0.424, Bhattacharyya upper bound 1.000.
- Cryptography: 6,000 Ed25519 sign/verify measurements across 128–1024-byte payloads; median sign ≈0.040–0.044 ms and verify ≈0.110–0.113 ms on this runner.
- Deceptive evidence: controlled substitution, timestamp alteration, signer-identity substitution, and replay tests are detected/rejected under the packaged threat model.
- Risk–coverage: 2,700 cases; full-coverage risk ≈0.00519; threshold 0.85 gives coverage ≈0.433 with zero observed selective error; AURC ≈0.00100.
- Identical Outcome Challenge: 300 paired incidents × 2 evidence conditions × 5 methods = 3,000 method-runs. Forced baselines have FAR_NI=1.0; TRACE-R has FAR_NI=0.0 by abstaining on deliberately non-identifiable cases.
- Responsibility transferability stress test: the simple JS channel-signature score is **not predictive** across six held-out families (Spearman ρ≈0.068, p≈0.899). This negative result is retained.
- External adapters: Who&When, AgentDojo, GRADE, MP-Bench/TraceElephant metadata, and related current baselines are documented. The offline validation environment could not download external corpora; no external-run accuracy is fabricated.
