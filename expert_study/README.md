# Expert evidentiary-sufficiency validation protocol

This folder contains a **preregisterable template**, not fabricated expert results. `vignettes.csv` has 60 balanced controlled vignettes. Experts should answer only whether the displayed evidence is sufficient to distinguish two pre-specified responsibility hypotheses; they are not asked to decide legal liability.

Recommended panel: cyber-law, digital-forensics/security, and AI-governance experts. Report Fleiss kappa or Krippendorff alpha plus association between expert sufficiency judgments and effective legal observability. Keep expert responses in a new file; do not overwrite generated ground-truth fields.
